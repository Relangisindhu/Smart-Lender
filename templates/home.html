How the Prediction Pipeline Works

1

Data Intake

Gender, marital status, education, employment, income, loan amount/term, credit history and property area.

2

Preprocessing

Missing value imputation, categorical encoding, outlier capping, SMOTE balancing and feature scaling.

3

Model Inference

A tuned XGBoost classifier (benchmarked against Decision Tree, Random Forest and KNN) scores the application.

4

Decision Output

Approval / rejection outcome with a confidence percentage and a breakdown of submitted factors.
